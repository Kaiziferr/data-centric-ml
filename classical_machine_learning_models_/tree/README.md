# Decision Trees

This section of the **data-centric-ml** repository focuses on the study and practical application of **Decision Tree** techniques within the field of **Machine Learning**.

It brings together a collection of experiments, methodologies, and strategies for:

- Building Decision Tree–based models
- Optimizing model performance
- Evaluating predictive performance and generalization
- Interpreting model behavior and feature importance
- Improving model robustness and overall performance

The goal is to explore **Decision Trees** from both a theoretical and practical perspective, with an emphasis on **data-centric approaches** to model development and evaluation.

## 01 — Pre-Pruning & Hyperparameter Search

Exploration of pre-pruning techniques through hyperparameter search and optimization to control tree complexity.

## 02 — Pre-Pruning & ccp_alpha Hyperparameter Search

Analysis of `ccp_alpha` as a parameter for controlling model complexity and preventing overfitting in Decision Trees.

## 03 — Post-Pruning

Exploration of post-pruning techniques, evaluating different pruning levels and their impact on model performance and complexity.
